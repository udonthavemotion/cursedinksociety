## Louisiana Tattoo Business Legal Requirements, Licensing, and Compliance (2024)

Operating a tattoo parlor in Louisiana requires strict adherence to state health and safety regulations to protect both artists and clients. The Louisiana Department of Health (LDH) is the primary regulatory body.

### 1. Governing Authority

*   **Louisiana Department of Health (LDH), Office of Public Health, Section of Environmental Public Health.**
*   **Relevant Regulations:** Louisiana Administrative Code (LAC) Title 48, Part V, Chapter 85, "Body Art Establishments."

### 2. Licensing Requirements

Both the **establishment (parlor)** and **individual artists** must be permitted by the LDH.

#### A. Body Art Establishment Permit (Facility License)

*   **Application:** A completed application form must be submitted to the LDH.
*   **Fees:** An annual permit fee is required (check current LDH fee schedules, typically around $250-$300 for a new permit, with renewal fees).
*   **Pre-Operational Inspection:** Before a permit is issued, the establishment must undergo and pass a pre-operational inspection by an LDH sanitarian to ensure compliance with all structural, equipment, and sanitation requirements.
*   **Renewal:** Permits must be renewed annually.
*   **Display:** The establishment permit must be prominently displayed in a public area within the facility.
*   **Change of Ownership/Location:** A new permit application and inspection are required for any change in ownership or location.

#### B. Body Art Artist Permit (Individual Artist License)

*   **Eligibility:**
    *   Must be at least 18 years of age.
    *   Must possess a current certificate of completion for a Bloodborne Pathogen (BBP) training course approved by the LDH. This training must be renewed annually.
*   **Application:** A completed application form must be submitted to the LDH.
*   **Fees:** An annual permit fee is required (check current LDH fee schedules, typically around $50-$75 for a new permit, with renewal fees).
*   **Renewal:** Permits must be renewed annually.
*   **Display:** The artist's permit must be prominently displayed at their workstation or in a public area within the establishment.
*   **Working in Multiple Establishments:** An artist may work in multiple permitted establishments, but their individual permit must be valid and displayed at each location.

### 3. Health Department Regulations (Sanitation & Safety)

LDH regulations are highly detailed regarding the physical environment, equipment, and operational procedures to prevent the spread of infectious diseases.

#### A. Facility Requirements

*   **Structure:**
    *   Floors, walls, and ceilings must be smooth, non-absorbent, easily cleanable, and in good repair.
    *   Adequate lighting must be provided in all work areas.
    *   Good ventilation is required to prevent condensation and odors.
*   **Work Areas:**
    *   Each artist must have a dedicated, separate work area that allows for proper sanitation and privacy.
    *   Work surfaces must be smooth, non-absorbent, and easily cleanable.
*   **Handwashing Facilities:**
    *   Dedicated handwashing sinks with hot and cold running water, liquid soap, and single-use paper towels or an air dryer must be available in or immediately adjacent to each work area.
    *   No handwashing sink can be used for cleaning instruments or disposing of waste.
*   **Restrooms:** Clean and accessible restrooms must be available for clients and staff.
*   **Waiting Area:** A separate waiting area from the tattooing area is required.
*   **Storage:** Separate, clean, and organized storage areas for clean instruments, sterile supplies, and personal belongings.

#### B. Equipment & Sterilization

*   **Sterilization Equipment:**
    *   An autoclave (steam sterilizer) is mandatory for all reusable instruments that penetrate the skin.
    *   The autoclave must be maintained and operated according to manufacturer specifications.
    *   **Spore Testing:** Autoclaves must be biologically spore-tested at least monthly by an independent laboratory to verify proper sterilization. Records of these tests must be maintained for at least two years.
*   **Single-Use Items:**
    *   All needles, razors, ink caps, gloves, and other items that come into contact with the client's skin or bodily fluids must be single-use, sterile, and disposed of immediately after use.
    *   Needles must be stored in individually sealed, sterile packages.
*   **Inks/Pigments:**
    *   Only non-toxic, sterile, pre-packaged, single-use inks/pigments are permitted.
    *   Bulk inks are generally prohibited unless dispensed into single-use containers.
    *   Ink caps must be single-use and disposed of after each client.
*   **Disinfectants:** Approved hospital-grade disinfectants must be used for cleaning surfaces and equipment.

#### C. Operational Procedures & Sanitation

*   **Hand Hygiene:** Artists must wash hands thoroughly with soap and water before and after each client, and whenever gloves are changed.
*   **Gloves:** New, disposable, single-use gloves must be worn by the artist during the entire tattooing procedure and changed if torn, contaminated, or between different stages of the procedure.
*   **Skin Preparation:** The client's skin must be thoroughly cleaned and disinfected prior to tattooing.
*   **Cross-Contamination Prevention:** Strict measures to prevent cross-contamination between clean and contaminated items, and between clients. This includes covering equipment, using barrier films, and proper disposal.
*   **Waste Disposal:**
    *   **Sharps:** All used needles and other sharp instruments must be immediately placed into an approved, puncture-resistant, leak-proof sharps container.
    *   **Biohazardous Waste:** Any materials saturated with blood or bodily fluids must be disposed of as biohazardous waste according to state and local regulations.
    *   **General Waste:** Regular trash must be kept in covered, leak-proof containers.
*   **Aftercare Instructions:** Clients must be provided with verbal and written aftercare instructions for the tattoo, including potential complications and signs of infection.

### 4. Age Restrictions

*   **Minimum Age:** In Louisiana, it is **illegal to tattoo any person under the age of 18**, regardless of parental consent.
*   **Identification:** Valid, government-issued photo identification (e.g., driver's license, state ID card, passport) must be presented by the client and verified by the artist prior to any procedure. A copy or record of the ID must be kept with the client's consent form.

### 5. Consent Forms

A comprehensive, written consent form is legally required for every client prior to receiving a tattoo. This form serves as a legal document acknowledging the client's understanding and acceptance of the procedure and its risks.

*   **Mandatory Information on the Consent Form:**
    *   **Client Information:** Full legal name, address, phone number, date of birth.
    *   **Identification Verification:** Type of ID presented, ID number, and issuing authority. A copy of the ID should be attached or scanned.
    *   **Health Disclosure:** A detailed questionnaire regarding the client's medical history, including but not limited to:
        *   Allergies (latex, metals, dyes, etc.)
        *   Skin conditions (eczema, psoriasis, keloids, moles, etc.)
        *   Bloodborne diseases (Hepatitis, HIV/AIDS)
        *   Heart conditions, pacemakers
        *   Diabetes
        *   Hemophilia or other bleeding disorders
        *   Epilepsy, fainting spells
        *   Pregnancy or nursing status
        *   Medications (especially blood thinners, immunosuppressants)
        *   Recent surgeries or medical procedures
    *   **Acknowledgment of Risks:** Explicit acknowledgment that the client understands the risks associated with tattooing, including:
        *   Pain and discomfort
        *   Infection
        *   Allergic reactions
        *   Scarring
        *   Permanent nature of the tattoo
        *   Potential interference with medical procedures (e.g., MRI)
        *   Difficulty or impossibility of removal
    *   **Aftercare Instructions:** A statement confirming the client has received and understands verbal and written aftercare instructions.
    *   **Client Signature & Date:** The client's legal signature and the date of signing.
    *   **Artist Signature & Date:** The artist's signature and the date the procedure was performed.
    *   **Witness Signature (Optional but Recommended):** For added legal protection, especially if there are any concerns or for minors (though minors cannot be tattooed in LA).
    *   **Design & Placement:** A description or diagram of the tattoo design and its exact placement on the body.
*   **Retention:** Signed consent forms and associated ID copies must be retained by the establishment for a minimum of **two years** from the date of the procedure.

### 6. Compliance Requirements & Best Practices

*   **Record Keeping:** Maintain meticulous records for:
    *   Client consent forms and ID copies (2 years minimum).
    *   Autoclave spore test results (2 years minimum).
    *   Bloodborne Pathogen training certificates for all artists (current and past).
    *   Waste disposal manifests (if applicable for biohazardous waste).
    *   Inspection reports from LDH.
*   **Inspections:** Be prepared for unannounced inspections by LDH sanitarians. They will review records, inspect the facility for cleanliness and compliance with all regulations, and observe procedures.
*   **Display of Permits:** Ensure both the establishment permit and all artist permits are prominently displayed.
*   **Signage:**
    *   Post clear signage regarding age restrictions ("Must be 18+ with valid ID").
    *   Consider posting health warnings or a summary of client responsibilities.
*   **Insurance:**
    *   **General Liability Insurance:** Essential to cover premises liability (e.g., client slips and falls).
    *   **Professional Liability (Malpractice) Insurance:** Highly recommended for artists and the establishment to cover claims related to the tattooing procedure itself (e.g., infection, allergic reaction).
*   **Local Ordinances:** In addition to state regulations, check with your specific city and parish for any local business licenses, zoning requirements, or additional health department rules that may apply (e.g., Houma, Terrebonne Parish).
*   **Bloodborne Pathogens Training:** Ensure all artists complete an LDH-approved BBP training course annually. This training is critical for understanding and preventing the transmission of infectious diseases.
