# CURSED INK SOCIETY WEBSITE REVAMP - PRODUCT REQUIREMENTS DOCUMENT

## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Cursed Ink Society is a premium tattoo collective in Houma, Louisiana, featuring a talented team of versatile tattoo artists. This PRD outlines the complete revamp of their website to reflect their unique brand identity, showcase their artistic excellence, and establish market dominance through superior digital presence.

## BUSINESS OBJECTIVES

- Establish Cursed Ink Society as the premier tattoo destination in Houma
- Create a digital experience that reflects the artistic excellence of the collective
- Build trust and credibility through professional presentation
- Generate high-quality leads through compelling portfolio showcase
- Position Cursed Ink Society as a premium brand in the competitive Houma market

## TARGET AUDIENCE

### Primary Audience: Tattoo Enthusiasts
- Age: 18-45
- Interests: Body art, personal expression, artistic design
- Values: Quality craftsmanship, artistic integrity, professional service
- Behavior: Research studios online, follow artists on social media, seek unique designs

### Secondary Audience: Professional Clients
- Age: 25-55
- Interests: Premium services, established artists, professional environment
- Values: Expertise, reliability, exceptional customer experience
- Behavior: Seek referrals, research reputation, prioritize quality over price

- **Booking Information**: Contact methods and availability

#### 3. Portfolio/Gallery (/gallery)

- **High-Resolution Images**: Professional photography standards
