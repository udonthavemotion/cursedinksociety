## 2. Healing Time Corrections

### **Ear Piercings**

- **Before:** `6-8 weeks`
- **After:** `6-8 weeks initial / 2-4 months complete`

## 7. Critical Professional Changes

### **Surface Bar vs Curved Barbell**
**This is the most important correction:**

Surface piercings MUST use proper surface bars (shaped like a staple), NOT curved barbells. This is a critical safety and longevity issue. Using curved barbells in surface piercings dramatically increases rejection rates.

### **LITHA Philosophy**
Added "LITHA (Leave It The Hell Alone)" to nose piercing aftercare.

This is industry standard language that helps clients understand the less-is-more approach to healing.

### **No Soaks, Use Spray**
Changed all "saline soaks" to "spray with sterile saline."

Modern APP guidance has moved away from soaks toward sterile saline spray products (like NeilMed) as they:
- Are more sterile
- Are easier to use
- Don't trap bacteria
- Don't irritate with temperature changes

### **Honest Language About Rejection**
Surface piercings and eyebrow piercings now have explicit language about:
- Migration risk
- Rejection being common/possible
- Need for quality jewelry
- Being prepared for possible failure

This is informed consent language that protects both piercer and client.
