# Luis Reymundo

## Overview
Luis Reymundo is a dedicated tattoo artist with a strong focus on traditional and Japanese tattooing. His work is characterized by bold lines, vibrant colors, and a deep respect for the rich history of these art forms. He is a key artist at Cursed Ink Society in Houma, Louisiana, and previously honed his craft at Another Realm Tattoo and Black Pearl Tattoo.

## Style Types
*   **Traditional American Tattooing:** Classic designs with strong black outlines, limited color palettes (often red, yellow, green, blue), and iconic imagery such as eagles, roses, anchors, and pin-ups.
*   **Japanese (Irezumi) Tattooing:** Large-scale, intricate designs often covering significant portions of the body. Common motifs include dragons, koi fish, Hannya masks, cherry blossoms, and samurai. Emphasis on flow, balance, and symbolic meaning.
