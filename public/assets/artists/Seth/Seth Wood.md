# Seth Wood

## Overview
Seth Wood is a tattoo artist associated with Cursed Ink Society in Houma, Louisiana. He has been tattooing since 1998 and has worked at various tattoo shops, including Best Day Ever Tattoo in Albany, CA, Temple Tattoo in Oakland, CA, and Memoir Tattoo. He was also associated with Black Pearl Tattoo in Houma, LA.
