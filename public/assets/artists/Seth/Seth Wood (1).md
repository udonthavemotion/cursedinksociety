# Seth Wood

## Overview
Seth Wood is a highly experienced tattoo artist with a career spanning since 1998. His work is a sophisticated blend of American Traditional and Japanese tattooing, interpreted with a naturalistic approach. He is now a key artist at Cursed Ink Society in Houma, Louisiana, bringing a wealth of experience from renowned studios like Best Day Ever Tattoo, Temple Tattoo, and Memoir Tattoo.

## Style Types
*   **Naturalistic American Traditional:** A modern interpretation of classic American Traditional tattoos, with more realistic shading, depth, and detail while maintaining the bold outlines and iconic imagery of the original style.
*   **Naturalistic Japanese:** A similar approach to Japanese tattooing, where traditional motifs are rendered with a greater sense of realism and natural flow, creating a more organic and less stylized look.
*   **Black and Grey:** As indicated by his @sethta2_bp Instagram bio, he is also proficient in black and grey tattooing, likely applying his naturalistic approach to create depth and dimension without color.
