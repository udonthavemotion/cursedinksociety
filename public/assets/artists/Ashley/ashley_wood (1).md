# Ashley Wood

## Overview
Ashley Wood is a vibrant and versatile tattoo artist specializing in color, newschool, and kawaii tattoos. Her work is characterized by its playful nature, bold colors, and often incorporates elements of pop culture and whimsical designs. She is a key artist at Cursed Ink Society in Houma, Louisiana, bringing a unique and cheerful aesthetic to the collective. She previously worked at Black Pearl Tattoo Houma.

## Style Types
*   **Color Tattoos:** Expert use of a broad and bright color palette to create dynamic and eye-catching tattoos.
*   **Newschool Tattoos:** Characterized by exaggerated features, bold outlines, and vibrant colors, often with a cartoonish or illustrative feel. This style allows for immense creativity and a unique interpretation of subjects.
*   **Kawaii Tattoos:** Focuses on cute, charming, and often simplified designs, drawing inspiration from Japanese pop culture. These tattoos are typically small, colorful, and evoke a sense of sweetness and innocence.
*   **Dotwork:** (from Tattoos Wizard) Utilizes small dots to create shading, texture, and intricate patterns, often used in conjunction with other styles.
*   **Sketch:** (from Tattoos Wizard) Tattoos that mimic the appearance of a pencil sketch, with visible lines and a raw, artistic feel.
*   **Geometric:** (from Tattoos Wizard) Incorporates geometric shapes, lines, and patterns to create abstract or stylized designs.
