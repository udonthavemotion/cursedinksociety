# Cody Crochet

## Overview
Cody Crochet is a highly skilled tattoo artist specializing in vibrant and detailed anime, illustrated, and video game tattoos. Known for his exceptional color work, Cody transforms pop culture references into dynamic and lasting art on skin. He is a key artist at Cursed Ink Society in Houma, Louisiana.

## Style Types
*   **Anime/Manga Tattoos:** Character portraits, full scenes, and iconic imagery from popular anime and manga series. Strong emphasis on accurate character representation and vibrant color palettes.
*   **Illustrated Tattoos:** Custom designs with a distinct artistic flair, often incorporating elements of fantasy, pop surrealism, and graphic novel aesthetics.
*   **Color Work:** Expert application of a wide spectrum of colors, utilizing shading and blending techniques to create depth and luminosity.
*   **Video Game Tattoos:** Iconic characters, symbols, and scenes from video games, rendered with precision and a keen eye for detail.
